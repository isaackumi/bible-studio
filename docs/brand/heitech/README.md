# HeiTech logo

HeiTech is the company that makes Church Studio. Its mark is the **Pixel H**: seven panes of a
3 × 3 grid make an H, and the centre pane is lit in the live orange. It is a close relative of
Church Studio's Multiview mark (panes, rounded corners, one orange pane), so the company and its
product read as one family, while the H keeps them apart.

The masters are made by `build.py` (`python3 build.py <folder>`): change the geometry there, not by
hand, so every file stays in step.

## Colour

| | HEX | RGB | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HeiTech Ink | `#17171B` | 23 23 27 | The panes and the wordmark |
| Live Orange | `#FA4F00` | 250 79 0 | The centre pane only (shared with Church Studio) |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | Panes and wordmark on dark backgrounds |

Orange is for the one centre pane. Never set the whole logo, or the wordmark, in orange. Print
matches to check on paper: Pantone 1655 C for the orange, Pantone Black 6 C for the ink.

## The wordmark

"HeiTech" is drawn, not typed: its strokes are as heavy as the gaps between the panes, its ends are
square, and its round letters are plain circles. Do not retype it in a font. In running text, the
name is written **HeiTech** (capital H and T).

## Clear space and size

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to **one pane** (the width of one square) on every side.
- Smallest sizes: horizontal logo **96 px** wide on screen (25 mm in print); symbol **16 px**,
  using `heitech-symbol-small.svg` below 32 px.

## Don't

- Recolour the panes, or light a pane other than the centre one.
- Rearrange, add or remove panes, or round them into circles.
- Stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows or gradients.
- Put the colour logo on busy photos or mid-tone backgrounds: use the reversed or one-colour version.
- Put the Church Studio mark and the HeiTech mark side by side at the same size. The product leads in
  the app; HeiTech signs off below ("Made by HeiTech").

## Notes

- Trademark clearance has not been checked. Run a search (USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO Global Brand Database)
  before registering the mark.
- No font licence is involved: every letter is drawn as a shape.
