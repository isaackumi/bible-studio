# Launch checklist

What Church Studio needs before selling to churches, what is already in place, and what only the
owner can do (accounts, signatures, legal details). Last reviewed 21 October 2026.

## Already in place

- **Policies on the website:** Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund and Cancellation Policy,
  Open-source licenses and Security pages (`/terms`, `/privacy`, `/refunds`, `/licenses`,
  `/security`), linked from the home page footer and from the app (Settings › Updates › Legal).
- **Privacy Policy covers** everything the service collects: check-ins, trials (including the
  two-day count per network address), licenses, purchases, accounts, error reports, support,
  hosting logs and staff access. It also lists processors and where they are, international
  transfers, legal bases, retention per kind of data, security, rights (EU/UK, Ghana, Nigeria,
  California), children, and cookies (none for advertising or analytics).
- **Terms cover** the license, acceptable use, payment through Paddle as Merchant of Record,
  your content and song/Bible licensing, connected services and AI output, updates, open-source
  components, liability, suspension, changes with 30 days' notice, governing law and consumer rights.
- **Open-source notices:** all 723 components and their licenses, generated by
  `node scripts/third-party.mjs` (run it again after changing dependencies). No license blocks
  selling: fonts are OFL, five Rust crates are MPL-2.0 used unchanged, the rest are permissive.
  Bundled Bibles are public domain, plus the Akuapem Twi Bible under CC BY-SA 4.0 with its credit.
- **Security contact:** `/.well-known/security.txt` (RFC 9116), kept current automatically.
- **Admin safety:** Cloudflare Access sign-in, roles, limits, approvals and an audit log
  (server/README.md › Admin).

## Blocking a launch

1. **Sign and notarize the Mac app.** Without it, macOS says the app "cannot be opened because
   the developer cannot be verified" and most churches will stop there.
   Join the Apple Developer Program (US$99 a year), create a *Developer ID Application*
   certificate, and add these repository secrets: `APPLE_CERTIFICATE`,
   `APPLE_CERTIFICATE_PASSWORD`, `APPLE_SIGNING_IDENTITY`, `APPLE_ID`, `APPLE_PASSWORD` (an
   app-specific password) and `APPLE_TEAM_ID`. Then the release workflow can sign and notarize.
2. **Sign the Windows installer.** Without it, Windows SmartScreen warns that the app is unknown.
   Buy a code-signing certificate (an OV certificate, or Azure Trusted Signing, which is the
   cheapest today) and add it to the release workflow.
3. **Turn on real payments.** In Paddle: finish seller verification, approve the domain
   `church-studio.heitechinc.com`, and create the four prices (Premium and Church, monthly and
   yearly). Put their real ids in `PADDLE_PRICES` in `server/wrangler.jsonc` (the current ones
   are placeholders), set `BILLING_PROVIDER` to `paddle` and `PADDLE_ENV` to `live`, and set the
   live secrets (`PADDLE_API_KEY`, `PADDLE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `PADDLE_CLIENT_TOKEN`).
4. **Fill in the company's legal details.** In `server/wrangler.jsonc`, set `COMPANY_NAME` to
   HeiTech's exact registered name, `COMPANY_ADDRESS` to its postal address and `COMPANY_COUNTRY`
   to the country it is registered in. The Privacy Policy names the data controller with them,
   and the Terms use the country for governing law.
5. **Have a lawyer read the policies** for the countries you sell in. They are written carefully
   and in plain language, but they are not legal advice.

## Strongly recommended before launch

- **Register as a data controller where required.** Ghana's Data Protection Act requires
  registration with the Data Protection Commission; Nigeria's NDPC registers controllers above
  its thresholds. If you sell to churches in the EU or UK without an office there, ask your lawyer
  whether you need a representative there (GDPR Article 27).
- **Accept the processors' data processing agreements** in their dashboards: Cloudflare, Resend,
  Sentry and Paddle. This is usually one click each.
- **Put Cloudflare Access in front of `/admin`** and set `ADMIN_USERS` and `ADMIN_ALERT_EMAIL`
  (server/README.md › Admin). Until then the admin page only lets you look.
- **Make sure someone reads `support@heitechinc.com`**, which also receives privacy requests,
  security reports and refund requests. Privacy requests must be answered within 30 days.
- **Check the name before spending on it.** The name and logo may change; run a trademark search
  in the countries you sell in before printing or advertising.
- **Keep the signing keys safe and backed up:** `~/.church-studio/license-signing.key` (licenses)
  and `~/.church-studio/updater.key` (updates). Losing the updater key means installed apps can
  no longer update; leaking the license key means anyone could make licenses.

## Nice to have

- A status page and a short "what to do if the screen goes dark on Sunday" help page.
- An accessibility statement once the app has been checked with VoiceOver and Narrator.
- Release 0.2.5 so the latest app fixes (browser name in check-ins, no check-ins from development
  builds) reach installed copies.
